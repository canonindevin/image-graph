Shortcuts

  • Arrows move selection · Shift+Arrows grow it
  • Enter zoom to selection · Esc clear
  • +/− zoom · 0 fit · 1 actual size
  • G grid on/off · S pan/select · O overlay

Grid a photo, then paint square by square

  1. Open a photo (or take one).
  2. Choose any rows × columns — 2×2, 5×2, 100×100.
  3. Tap a square and zoom right into it.
  4. Switch to Overlay to see the photo through your camera, on your paper.
